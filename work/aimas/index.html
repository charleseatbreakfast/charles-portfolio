From fragmented campaign tools to one connected workspace.

The challenge

Campaign work was fragmented across disconnected workflows.

Planning, execution and performance management lived across separate tools and views, making it difficult to understand a campaign as one connected system.

What we learned

Three observations shaped the product structure.

The source research and workflow observation surfaced three recurring product problems.

  1. 01

    Journeys were managed without enough campaign-level context.

    A flat journey list made it difficult to see how related work belonged together.

  2. 02

    Complex logic became harder to follow across disconnected steps.

    Triggers, conditions, actions and waits needed to be understood as one journey.

  3. 03

    Performance information needed to stay connected to the campaign being managed.

    Campaign data was more useful when it remained part of the working context.

Product architecture

From separate campaign tools to one connected orchestration system.

The product was reframed around Campaign as a shared context, connecting related audience, journey-building and performance work without treating them as isolated tools.

Key design decision

Campaign as the container.

Campaign became the shared product context for organising related journeys, segments, execution status and performance in one place.

Product value

Related workflows became easier to understand together, while users could still move into journey-level detail without losing campaign context.

Key design decision

Keep performance visible in context.

The reporting experience used hierarchy, filtering and interactive data details to make campaign performance easier to interpret without turning AIMAS into a separate dashboard product.

Visual foundation

A consistent interface language supported a complex platform.

Colour, typography and reusable components created a clear foundation for campaign management, canvas controls and performance views.

Future direction · Concept

Exploring guided AI orchestration.

The early concept evolved from an open conversational prompt toward guided steps paired with live canvas feedback. This remained a conceptual future direction rather than shipped functionality.

Reflection

The biggest shift was not a new dashboard or canvas—it was giving campaign work a shared product structure.

Campaign context, journey logic and performance could then be understood as parts of the same system.
